# What is a game Jam?

A Game Jam is a comepetition where many individuals, given a theme, are encouraged to build a game. This theme is generally up to interpretation and allows the participants to truly build something unique.


## Personal note:

This is Enrique, the CS Club president for the 2026 school year. I would like to thank both the Game Development club and and the CS Club for coming together and organizing this event. We are really excited to see what students come up, given our theme, which we thing is pretty representative of Humboldt and something students can enjoy participating in.

# Bugs n' Slugs

The theme for this game jam is: *Bugs n' slugs*.

Why? We originally thought of banana slugs, and the time of planning, they were very frequent in my front yard and we had to come up with a silly name. So myself and the president of the Game Dev club, Jacob, agreed on Bugs n' Slugs.
