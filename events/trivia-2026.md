# About

This was a casual meeting, getting back into the swing of school again in the new semester. Led by Nate, we had a great time with peers discoving how many things we forgot, didn't know, or were suprized when our friend new something unexpected.

The rules dictated groups of 2 people (pair programming style). Questions are prefixed by a difficulty [`-1`,`1`,`2`,`3`,`4`], and `-1` is not CS-related trivia.
